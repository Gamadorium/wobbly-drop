Wobbly Drop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Draft for pre-release: This policy describes the planned standalone prototype only. Review and update it before integrating TikTok services, analytics, advertising, payments, or releasing the game.

1. About the game

Wobbly Drop is a casual physics-based stacking game. This document applies to the pre-release game prototype.

2. Information handled by the prototype

The planned prototype does not require account registration or submission of personal details. Game settings and high scores may be stored locally on your device or browser. The hosting service may process technical request data such as IP addresses and browser information according to its own policies.

3. Advertising, analytics and platform integrations

The planned prototype does not include advertising, analytics or payment SDKs. If these services or TikTok platform features are added, this policy must be revised before release to explain the providers, data collected, purposes and applicable choices.

4. Children

We do not intentionally request personal information from children in the prototype. The game will be operated in accordance with the age requirements of its distribution platform.

5. Data retention and choices

Locally saved game progress can generally be removed by clearing the relevant browser or application storage. Hosting provider logs are managed by the provider under its policies.

6. Changes

We may update this policy as the game develops. The revision date above will be updated.
